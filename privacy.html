Privacy Policy for Rounds

Operated by TGW Software • Effective Date: September 26, 2026

TGW Software ("we", "our", or "us") provides Rounds, a private circle check-in and nightlife safety coordination application. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, store, and process your personal information when using our mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

To deliver core social and safety coordination features, Rounds collects the following categories of data:

  • Account Identification: When registering, we collect your email address, username, and generate a unique 6-character user code (Short ID) to enable peer-to-peer circle invitations without sharing private contact information.
  • Circle & Activity Data: Check-in event timestamps, venue names, drink count pacing records, and safe arrival status transitions submitted during active sessions.
  • Device Push Tokens: Hardware and Expo push notification tokens required to deliver real-time arrival alerts, venue check-ins, and circle invites across your trusted groups.
  • Location Data (Optional): If explicitly permitted, foreground location coordinates are accessed solely to surface nearby venue suggestions upon manual check-in. Rounds does not run continuous background location tracking or sell location history.

2. Purpose and Usage of Data

Collected information is strictly used for the following operational requirements:

  • Facilitating closed-loop Circle rosters and displaying live venue statuses among approved members.
  • Dispatching push notifications for check-in events and safe home arrival alerts.
  • Generating session drink pacing summaries and historical nightlife receipts.
  • Preventing abuse, maintaining session security, and authenticating user identities.

3. Third-Party Infrastructure

Data collected through Rounds is processed and stored using industry-standard backend infrastructure:

  • Supabase: Encrypted PostgreSQL database hosting, row-level security (RLS), and authentication services.
  • Expo Application Services (EAS): Routing encrypted push notification payloads to Apple Push Notification service (APNs) and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM).

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

4. Data Retention and Account Deletion

In accordance with Apple App Store Guideline 5.1.1 and Google Play User Data policies, users maintain full control over their personal data:

  • In-App Deletion: You can permanently delete your account and all associated data directly within the application by navigating to Settings > Delete Account.
  • Immediate Purge: Initiating deletion immediately wipes your profile, circle memberships, historic receipts, active check-ins, and push tokens from our primary database tables.
  • Manual Requests: You may also request account erasure by contacting our privacy coordinator at support@tgwsoftware.com.

5. Security

All data transmissions between the Rounds mobile client and our servers are encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). Database access is protected by granular row-level security policies ensuring circle activity is accessible only by verified members of that circle.
